## Art direction

The reference is the third design concept selected in the conversation: a dark, premium royal asset shop gallery with black/obsidian backgrounds, warm metallic gold structures, red velvet, ruby gemstones, 3D crowns, wings, crystal jewelry, cinematic reflections, and golden particles.

- Base palette: obsidian `#0A080E`, dark walnut `#231317`, deep velvet `#4A061B`.
- Metallic accents: antique gold `#B8823D`, royal gold `#F5C76B`, bright highlight `#FFF2C8`.
- Jewel accents: ruby `#B60F32`, sapphire `#4496E8`, violet `#B963EC`.
- Sculpted, physically convincing 3D forms rather than flat geometrical vector silhouettes.
- Realistic depth, directional spotlights, ambient occlusion, subtle bloom and high-resolution texture work.
- Maintain a consistent dark premium look across frames, entrances, gifts, medals and SBF Nobels.

## Integration acceptance criteria

1. **Independent files, not a sliced collage.** The concept illustration is a design reference; each production item must be rendered/exported separately.
2. **Profiles and seats remain readable.** Transparent frames sit outside the avatar circle; frame graphics must not cover the speaker photo or mic/seat controls.
3. **Real video and sound.** Do not label an SVG animation or static PNG a sound-enabled video gift.
4. **Mobile-tested** alpha, resolution, memory use, frame timing, audio policy and app playback before enabling in Sharika/Sura Chat.
5. **Licensing recorded** for every third-party template or sound, and source files not redistributed without permission.
6. Do not modify or remove the 12 Game Center games or their server-authoritative practice wallet when adding the asset library.
7. Source manifest must track `id`, `category`, `file`, `mime`, `size`, `animated`, `audio`, `coinPrice`, `license`, `status`, `version`, `lastVerified`.
